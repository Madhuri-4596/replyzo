What owners ask us first.

Isn't this just a WhatsApp tool I could buy?

You can buy the tool. Most owners never set it up, write the messages, or check if it works. We do all of that and answer for the result every month.

Will it sound like a robot?

We write every message in your tone, in English or Hinglish. The AI only answers questions you approve, and passes anything unusual to your team.

Do I need to change my software?

Often you can keep your current tools. We assess your Shopify store, forms, ads, lead portals or Google Sheets and confirm available integrations before you pay for setup.

What if it doesn't work?

We agree on a baseline, success measures and review schedule before kickoff. Results depend on lead quality, your offer and your team. Any service commitments or remedies are set out in your written agreement.

How long until it's live?

Typically around seven days after we receive access, approved messages and the required platform approvals. Integration complexity can change the timeline.

Is my customer data safe?

We plan workflows around your approved WhatsApp Business setup, customer opt-ins and opt-outs. Access, integrations and data handling are agreed before onboarding. Your team reviews messages before launch.
